🧭 1. Best practice (recommended)
✔ Step 1: Fill your part digitally (PC)

👉 You should:

type your section (name, nationality, etc.) using PDF or Word
keep it clean and readable
✔ Step 2: Send to company
send by email (PDF format)
they will print it
✔ Step 3: Guarantor fills manually

They will:

write their name (handwritten)
sign / stamp (印鑑)
fill address / company info by hand
✔ Step 4: Return original
via letter pack or scan + original later
🧠 2. Why this method is best in Japan

Because:

🟢 Your part:
must be clean and error-free → digital is better
🟢 Guarantor part:
must be handwritten / stamped → legal proof
⚠️ 3. What NOT to do

❌ writing everything by hand (messy, risk of mistakes)
❌ sending only handwritten scanned copy
❌ letting company fill entire form without structure
❌ using only digital signature (not accepted for PR guarantor)

🧭 4. Simple rule
Section	Method
Your part	Digital (typed) ✔
Guarantor name	Handwritten ✔
Guarantor signature	Handwritten / stamp ✔
